## My Recommendation

**Option 1 (Graphite & Copper)** if you want to build a **gadget brand** that nobody else has. The copper is bold, memorable, and your white/silver products will look incredible against graphite.

**Option 2 (NAMA Twin)** if you want to **copy what works** and focus on execution over identity. Green/gold/cream is proven in COD. It may feel slightly "wellness" for gadgets, but it's distinctive and converts.

**Option 3 (Charcoal & Champagne)** if you want to feel like **Apple in Mexico**. Minimal, expensive, quiet. Safe. Maybe too safe.

**Option 4 (Navy & Sand)** if you want **trust first, brand second**. Navy is the grown-up blue. It won't scare anyone. But it also won't surprise anyone.
