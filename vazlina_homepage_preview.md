## How It Feels

- **Not a generic Shopify store.** It feels like a real brand took time to design this.
- **Not aggressive.** No red countdown timers, no "BUY NOW OR DIE" language.
- **Trust-first.** Every section answers "Why should I believe you?"
- **Clean.** Lots of white space. Easy to read. Easy to scroll.
- **Mobile-first.** Every section stacks perfectly on phone.
