# Copywriting Brief — Vazlina Website Content Update

## Mission
Improve ALL text inside existing sections. **Do NOT change structure, layout, or design.** Only rewrite words.

## Core Rule
**Customers do NOT buy products. They buy:**
- Better outcomes
- Solutions
- Comfort
- Protection
- Confidence
- Peace of mind
- Beauty
- Convenience

---

## Product 1: Vazlina Brisa (Portable Fan)

### Mindset Shift
❌ "Portable fan that blows air"
✅ **"Personal comfort companion. Portable climate control. Portable relief."**

### What Brisa Really Sells
Protection from heat. Comfort for family. Peace of mind.

### Real-Life Scenarios
Metro, bus, walking, shopping, university, outdoor work, parents with babies, strollers, school pickup, parks, outdoor events, hot office.

### Emotional Transformation
**Before:** Sweaty → Uncomfortable → Irritated → Exhausted
**After:** Fresh → Comfortable → Relaxed → Confident → In Control

### Writing Rules for Brisa
- Never describe "3 speeds" or "battery life" first.
- Always start with the **feeling** or **situation**.
- Parents: "Your baby stays cool. You stay calm."
- Office workers: "Arrive fresh, not sweaty."
- Students: "Focus on your notes, not the heat."

## Product 2: Vazlina Guardián (Smart Charger)

### Mindset Shift
❌ "Phone charger"
✅ **"Protection device. Safety device. Peace of mind device."**

### What Guardián Really Sells
Safety. Family protection. Worry-free sleep.

### Real-Life Scenarios
Charging overnight, forgetting phone plugged in, parents worried about kids, elderly family, families wanting safer homes.

### Emotional Transformation
**Before:** Worry → Risk → Stress → Forgetfulness
**After:** Safety → Protection → Confidence → Peace of mind

### Powerful Angles
- "You sleep. Guardián stays awake."
- "Even if you forget, Guardián protects."
- "Protect your family. Protect your phone. Protect your investment."

### Writing Rules for Guardián

- Family angle is stronger than tech angle.

## Product 3: Vazlina Mariposa (Wireless Earbuds)

### Mindset Shift
❌ "Wireless earbuds for music"

### What Mariposa Really Sells
Style. Beauty. Confidence. Multiple identities. Self-expression.

### Real-Life Scenarios
Dates, cafes, shopping, social events, university, gym, travel, daily walks.

### Removable Wings = Versatility
- Elegant Mode (wings on)
- Fashion Mode (wings on, different color)
- Minimalist Mode (wings off)

### Emotional Transformation
**Before:** Ordinary → Generic → Forgettable
**After:** Elegant → Stylish → Unique → Confident

## Universal Rules for ALL Pages

### What to Change
- **Headlines** → Sell the outcome, not the feature.
- **Subheadlines** → Describe the transformation.
- **Product descriptions** → Start with emotion, end with feature proof.
- **Trust badges** → "Garantía 30 días" = "Sleep easy for 30 days."
- **FAQ answers** → Include emotional reassurance, not just facts.
- **Comparison blocks** → Vazlina = comfort/confidence/style. Others = risk/boring/ordinary.
- **Guarantees** → "Devolución sin drama" = "Zero risk. Full confidence."
- **CTA buttons** → "Ver la Colección" → "Encuentra tu alivio" / "Protege tu familia" / "Exprésate hoy"

### Tone
- Warm, human, Mexican Spanish.
- Speak like a trusted friend, not a salesman.
- "Nosotros" (we) when talking about the brand.
- "Tú" (you) when talking to the customer.
- No fake urgency. No "compra ya o pierdes."
- Every sentence should answer: **"So what? How does this improve my life?"**

### Quick Test
Read any line. Ask: **"Would a customer actually care about this?"**
If it only describes a feature ("3 velocidades") without the benefit ("para que elijas tu brisa perfecta"), rewrite it.

## Priority Order
1. **Homepage Hero** — most visitors see this first
2. **Homepage Product Cards** — where people click
3. **Product Page Hero** — where people decide
4. **Product Page Problem/Solution** — where people feel understood
5. **CTAs and Buttons** — where people take action
6. **FAQ** — where objections get destroyed

**Do NOT redesign. Only rewrite.**
